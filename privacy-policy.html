Brain Rush Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 18, 2026

Brain Rush is a mobile game featuring mini-games and offline progress tracking. This policy explains how information may be handled when you use the app.

Information Stored on Your Device

Game progress, scores, levels, and related statistics may be stored locally on your device. This information is used to provide game features.

Advertising

Brain Rush uses Google AdMob to display advertisements. Google and its advertising partners may collect or process information such as device identifiers, IP address, diagnostics, and advertising interaction data to provide, measure, and protect advertising, subject to applicable settings and consent requirements.

Third-Party Services

Google's services are governed by Google's own privacy policies. Learn more at Google Privacy Policy.

Data Sharing

Brain Rush does not operate a developer-owned account system or game-progress server, based on the current app design. Information handled by third-party advertising services is subject to their policies and settings.

Children's Privacy

Brain Rush is not intended to collect personal information directly from children. Advertising and age-related settings should be configured in accordance with applicable laws and Google Play requirements.

Security and Changes

No method of storage or transmission is completely secure. This policy may be updated when the app or its data practices change.
